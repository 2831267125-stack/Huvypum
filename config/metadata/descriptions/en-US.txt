Huvypum is a board for short music performance clips. Watch stage takes from other performers, keep the ones you like, and put your own liner-note clip up for the room.

Board opens on full-width concert stills from indie rooms, jazz trios, booth sets, and outdoor kits. Tap a card to read its liner note, follow the performer, or leave a comment on the take. A filter at the top of the board hides spam, promo bait, and off-topic clips when you want a cleaner scroll.

Booth is where a take comes together. Pick a performance video from your library or record a new fragment on the spot, then type the liner note or speak it. Try the preview as many times as you want; nothing reaches the board until you confirm it.

Saving a finished clip costs one Spotlight. A few Spotlights come with the app, and more are sold in packs in the Store under Me.

Nights groups clips by venue bill, so you can open a room and see every take from that night. Your own clips and your Spotlight balance collect on Me, next to your display name and photo.

If a clip or a performer is out of line, report the clip or block the performer from the card's more menu. The community guidelines and a way to reach us sit in the same place.
