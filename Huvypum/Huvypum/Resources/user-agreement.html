Clauses

The board

Huvypum is a music performance clip board on this iPhone. You watch takes, leave notes, and save your own liner-note clips. It is not a live venue or a messaging app. You use Huvypum at your own discretion.

Your content

You own the performance clips, liner notes, and comments you add on this iPhone. Keep them on this device. Delete Account removes clips, comments, Board state, and remaining Spotlights on this iPhone.

Spotlights and paid extras

Saving a finished clip spends 1 Spotlight. Photos, Record, liner notes, and preview do not spend Spotlights. Spotlight Packs are consumable, one-time App Store purchases. Apple's terms apply to payment. Spotlights stay on this iPhone.

Community notes

Keep Board about music performance clips and craft. Do not post hate, off-topic promo, or spam. Report a clip from the more menu. Block a performer from their profile.

Account

Leave Session keeps clips on this iPhone. Delete Account removes your profile, videos, comments, and remaining Spotlights. Questions: support@huvypum.app.
