Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 17, 2026

Clauses

What stays on this iPhone

Huvypum stores performance clips, liner notes, comments, Spotlights, and Board state on your iPhone. We do not operate a member server for this app.

Permissions

Huvypum uses the camera to capture a performance clip when you tap Record. Huvypum uses the photo library to choose an existing performance take when you tap Photos, or a profile photo when you tap your photo on Me. Huvypum uses the microphone to record a spoken liner note when you tap Voice note, and to record room sound when you tap Record. Tracking permission is requested so we can respect your choice, for example when you save a performance clip on Board. Notifications can remind you about an unfinished clip you allow.

Purchases

Spotlight Pack purchases are consumable. Spotlights stay on this iPhone. Removing the app or using Delete Account removes remaining Spotlights.
